# Date Night

A private app for one person that finds one new date a week with their partner in London, remembers every past date so none repeats, and learns what the couple enjoys.

## Language

### People

**Owner**:
The one person who uses the app and plans the dates.
_Avoid_: user, admin

**Partner**:
The person the Owner takes on dates. The Partner never sees the app.
_Avoid_: girlfriend, guest, second user

### Ideas and dates

**Idea**:
One thing the couple could do together: a venue, an activity at a venue, or a dated event, with its price, area and link.
_Avoid_: suggestion, activity, event, option

**Activity kind**:
The type of thing an Idea is, independent of where it happens, such as "pottery class" or "immersive dinner show".
_Avoid_: category, type, tag

**Category**:
One of the broad families of Ideas the taste is measured in: shows and immersive, food and drink, hands-on classes, active and outdoors.
_Avoid_: genre, kind

**Date**:
An Idea booked for one Date evening. A Date is done only when the Owner confirms it happened.
_Avoid_: booking, outing, plan

**Past date**:
A done Date, whether it came through the app or was found in the Owner's calendar, bank transactions or photos and confirmed by the Owner.
_Avoid_: history item, previous booking

**Repeat**:
An Idea at the venue of a Past date, or, for classes and experiences, of the same Activity kind as a Past date. A Repeat is never offered.
_Avoid_: duplicate

**Favourite**:
A Past date the Owner rated 5 out of 5. A Favourite is offered again only when the Owner asks for one.
_Avoid_: go-back list, classic

**Splurge**:
An Idea that costs more than £80 for the couple. At most one Splurge is booked each calendar month.
_Avoid_: treat, special date

### The week

**Date evening**:
The evening a Date takes place: Tuesday or Wednesday from 19:00, else Monday or Friday. Never Thursday.
_Avoid_: slot, date night, booking time

**Shortlist**:
The three Ideas offered to the Owner on Monday for a Date evening in the following week.
_Avoid_: picks, recommendations, digest

**Skipped week**:
A week with no Shortlist, because the Owner or the Partner travels or no Date evening is free.
_Avoid_: off week, pause

**Scout run**:
The weekly search of London listing sites that adds new Ideas.
_Avoid_: crawl, scrape, refresh

**Saved idea**:
An Idea the Owner found and sent in, such as a reel, a tip from a friend or an article.
_Avoid_: bookmark, manual idea

**Surprise hold**:
The calendar invitation the Partner gets for a Date: the time, the rough area and the dress code, never the venue.
_Avoid_: invite, calendar block

**Rating**:
The Owner's score from 1 to 5 for a done Date, with one line on what the couple liked.
_Avoid_: review, feedback, score
