# Past dates are proposed from evidence and confirmed by the Owner

The repeat rule needs the dates that came before the app. No single source lists them. The weekly history refresh collects evidence from three sources: evening calendar events, card spends of £20 or more in Actual Budget, and evening photos in Immich. It merges the evidence by day into possible past dates. The Owner confirms or dismisses each one on the History page. Only a confirmed day becomes a Past date. We rejected an automatic import, because a work dinner and a date leave the same evidence.

## Consequences

- Evenings away on a trip in TripIt never become possible past dates, so a holiday does not fill the list.
- A decided day keeps its decision when a later refresh finds more evidence for it.
